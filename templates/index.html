Our Story

A small kitchen with a big heart

Didi ki Rasoi started in one home kitchen with a simple idea: everyone deserves to eat meals that taste like they were made by someone who cares. Today, a group of Didis cook daily menus using family recipes passed down for generations.

We use seasonal vegetables, hand-ground masalas and traditional methods. Every meal supports a woman earning her own livelihood with dignity.

  • Hand-ground masalas, no artificial colours
  • Less oil, less spice, more taste
  • Pure veg kitchen
